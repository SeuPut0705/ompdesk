`runAlive` is the other execution, not the selected last idle execution. `saved` includes the other saved record. The mutations stop that execution, delete that record, or detach the other view so the preservation invariant reads observable outcomes.

The model preserves the real distributed boundary. Source final validation and source ACK happen before target local commit, and the source can change or expire after the ACK. That trace is permitted. It is not evidence that source and target form one atomic transaction. Target connection and view checks remain adjacent to target local commit.

Assumptions include a single in-flight request, opaque exact identities, authenticated binding of the original caller and challenged target, abstract provenance decisions, and a cooperative event-loop turn around the synchronous target commit. Authentication and cryptography are not proved. Individual local cleanup awaits are aggregated, and OS persistence is outside the model. The model currently cannot decide whether a counterexample is reproducible in production.
