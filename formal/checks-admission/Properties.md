# Frozen properties: checks-admission

Frozen at: 14 Nov 2026, from Main's brief (dispatch d1) and `docs/design/check-control.md` §5, §8, §9 and §10, before the model was first checked. Any change to a definition between the `BEGIN FROZEN PROPERTIES` and `END FROZEN PROPERTIES` banners of `tla/ChecksAdmission.tla`, to a constraint, or to `Fairness` is a **model change**: report it with the old text, the new text and the evidence. Never make one to turn a failing check green.

All properties are protocol level: any implementation of the hub's admission must hold them.

`Fairness` assumes that the pump and lease sweep run, a reserved intent is persisted, a dispatch receives an accepted or definitely refused answer when that answer remains enabled, every known run ends, and a cancellation is observed. It does not require an unconfirmed reservation to reconcile. `NoForeverUnconfirmed` is a separate environment assumption used only by `FairSpec` for P10. P11 itself does not need it. Nothing assumes that starts, lease lapses, or owner pause occur.

## Model changes after freezing

14 Nov 2026, Main's F1 decision (dispatch d6): a release entry whose own pool is full holds one account place; jobs behind it may not take it.

- P9 old: `FirstInLine(j) == Waiting(j) /\ Free(j) /\ ~(\E r : Waiting(r) /\ Competes(r, j) /\ Ahead(r, j) /\ Free(r))`. New: the same with `FreeFor` (a free place, less the account places held by release entries ahead of `j`) in place of `Free`. Evidence: under the hold rule the old P9 fails by design (a lower job sees a free account place that a release entry ahead of it holds); the amendment keeps every other case of P9, and its two mutations (`a-lapsed-entry-blocks-the-queue`; `strict-head-of-line`, now on the 3-place account of `MC_account3.cfg`, because on an account of 2 the one held place is the account's only spare and strict head-of-line means the same as the hold) and a new one (`hold-counts-for-entries-ahead`, on `MC_account_two_release.cfg`) still have to be detected.
- P12 added (above). P3 and P4 are unchanged: a higher or earlier job with a free place is never behind a held place a lower job could use, because every hold ahead of it is also ahead of the lower job.
- `KeepsLastAccountPlace` (the known-limit run of F1) is removed. It said only that a lower-lane job may take the account's last place while a release entry waits, which the hold rule now forbids for release entries; it never showed a release job blocked (see F1 in `SCOPE.md`).

14 Nov 2026, review of the integrated tree: the dispatch's answer is its own state (`sent`), and a run exists only once its dispatch was accepted (`launched`). The model had let a superseded entry's run be seen ending even when its dispatch then failed, which hid that the code kept such an entry `cancelling` with its place taken for good. `DispatchFails` now ends a `cancelling` entry whose dispatch failed, `CancelLands` needs a launched run, and `MC_live_supersede.cfg` checks P10 over it. No property changed; the mutation `a-dispatch-failing-after-a-supersede-keeps-its-place` (the code before the fix) must break P10.

14 Nov 2026, Main approved persistent `Unconfirmed` after an ambiguous write and expressly excluded permanent blockage from the release-liveness promise. The P10 formula is unchanged. Its old specification was `FairSpec == Spec /\ Fairness`, where a timeout was a failure that released capacity. Its new specification is `FairSpec == Spec /\ Fairness /\ NoForeverUnconfirmed`, where `NoForeverUnconfirmed == \A j \in Governed : [] (state[j] = "unconfirmed" => <> (state[j] # "unconfirmed"))`. The latter is an explicit environment assumption, not fairness for a reconciliation action or a claim that an outcome will arrive. Without it, an ambiguous predecessor can occupy the only place forever and the original unconditional P10 is false. This is the authorized exception, not a claim that TLC proved unconditional release progress.

The old `DispatchFails` combined definite refusal and timeout and released the place for both. It now handles only a definite refusal. `DispatchUnconfirmed` and `RecoverIntent` retain a place. A saved exact receipt can reconcile on recovery, but an unconfirmed intent without one has no reconciliation transition in this model. Raw completion no longer creates a green reuse source. P13 and P14 are new frozen safety properties. The failure and recovery cases and their mutations are specified but have not been run in this edit.

Sanity (each must be violated, so the model reaches what the properties are about): `NeverAdmits`, `NeverWaitsForAPlace`, `NeverExpires`, `NeverRefuses`, `NeverReuses`, `NeverCancelsARunningJob`, `NeverFillsTheAccount`, `NeverHoldsAnAccountPlace`, `NeverBlockedByLaterAdmit` (two outside racers can still take a held place, so P12 is not vacuous).
