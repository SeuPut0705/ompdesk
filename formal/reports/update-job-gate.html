F3 · Documented residual: a lease that expires before the quit opens the gate again

low suspected violated property: NoJobCutByQuit

If it fires after omp o1 answered frozen and before main's quit (during the 3 s answers window, or the installer start, seconds on Windows), a call of o1 passes the gate, registers a job, and the quit cuts it. The model has no clock: the passing runs assume it does not fire in that window (LeaseOutlastsRound=TRUE, assumption A2); this trace is what happens when the assumption fails. Whether the real timings allow it was not measured.

Model scope and assumptions

Modeled

  • JobGate enter/leave/agentEnded/freeze/thaw and the lease (job-gate.ts, composer.ts freeze_jobs/thaw_jobs/hooks)
  • main's freeze round: request, answers one by one, timeout, decide (install / thaw partial / hold), quit (background-work.ts, app-auto-update.ts #safeToInstall, updater.ts)
  • a tool call under way registering a job

Abstracted

  • each omp is single-threaded; freeze_jobs's tree read and gate decision are one step
  • main's decision and the tick that follows are one step
  • messages in order and reliable; the lease has no clock
  • jobs are a count; members fixed during the round

Out of scope

  • omps that become ready during the 3 s window
  • the tick's other holds, the user's postpone, idle(), a lost thaw request
  • relaunch continuing the cut turn, the installer

Assumptions

  • A1: an omp handles requests in order (a thaw cannot overtake a later freeze)
  • A2: the 20 s lease outlasts the round (3 s timeout) and the install start (seconds on Windows)
  • A5: a job starts only from a tool call that passed the gate
