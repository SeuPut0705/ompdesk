F1 · A new tab whose omp crashed before naming its session could come back on another conversation

low fixed violated property: NeverAnother

A new tab starts: its omp boots on a new session (n1). The ready frame clears #isNew (a10eb6877 did it in #handleReady). The omp crashes before the ready status's get_state answer reaches #observeResponse, so #sessionPath is still empty. The crash restart's sessionOpen then sees no session and a tab that is not new, and passes neither --session nor --config fresh-launch.yml: with the person's autoResume on, stock reopens the folder's most recent session (s1) in the tab that showed a new one. Fix: #isNew is cleared where get_state names the session, not at ready.
