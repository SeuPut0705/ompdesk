stock-lane verification

Design check of the stock helper lane as it moved into the core (native-rust 6b), then tied to the code. All seven frozen properties hold within 2 requests, 3 workers and 3 starts on a lane of each kind, and within 3 requests, 2 workers and 2 starts on the stock lane; 11 of 11 mutations are detected. No counterexample was found, so there is no finding to reproduce. Two design points came from the model: start numbers come from one process-wide sequence, so an answer to a start asked before a stop is never taken for a later start's, and a stop forgets the start it asked for.

Model scope and assumptions

Modeled

  • Lane::request with the retire of a worker that outlived its omp
  • the start asked of main (stock.launch) and its late, failed or missing answer
  • the request's timeout in its handler and Lane::gave_up
  • Lane::line (a worker's answer, read by its reader thread, handled by the lane)
  • Lane::overdue (the stock worker ended as hung)
  • Lane::idle (the extensions worker's idle exit)
  • a worker that answers in any order, never answers, or exits at any moment
  • Lane::stop and a lane used again after it
  • omp replaced at any moment

Abstracted

  • one lane; the table of lanes and its lock are not modelled
  • the fresh lane after a stop is the same lane, emptied
  • timers fire at any moment after they were armed

Out of scope

  • the worker's own queue and withdrawal (resources/gui-bridge/stock-helper/dispatch.ts)
  • time: how long a grace or an idle exit is, the idle epoch
  • the wait for exits at a stop, the workers ending with the core
  • liveness
  • main's side of stock.launch

Assumptions

  • A1: the lane takes one message at a time (one task, one inbox) and is the only one to answer a caller
  • A2: start numbers never repeat in the process (NEXT_ID)
