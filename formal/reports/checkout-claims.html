F2 · A failed call gave back a claim main had judged committed

medium fixed violated property: CommittedIsNobodys

A edited F; the person committed it. While main waits for git, A starts an edit of F that will fail: the hook claims F with a new stamp and remembers the committed claim as the one it took over. Main retires the committed stamp; the ledger keeps F (a call writes it). The edit fails and reverts F to the claim it took over, the committed one. When B then changes F, A's book holds a claim on B's change; only main's memory of the commit hides it, and a restart of main loses that.

Reproduction: src/main/checkout-peers.test.ts 'never gives back a committed claim when the call that took it over fails' before the fix: fails; after: passes

Fix: ClaimBook.retire voids the taken-over claim of a running call when that claim is the one retired.. Model re-check: passes; mutation check: fails-as-expected
