## UI parity

### Parity sheet: this computer, a paired computer, the phone
- **A UI change's final report attaches the parity sheet of every surface it touched** (the owner's rule, 3 Nov 2026, after agents kept checking only their own window): `bun run parity -- <surface…>` (`scripts/parity.ts`, `e2e/parity.e2e.ts`) captures the same session of one computer three ways (its own desktop window at 1280×860, another OMPdesk paired with it viewing that session from its section of the sidebar, and its phone page at 390×844 in Chromium), en light on the fake omp, and puts them side by side in `<dir>/<surface>/sheet.png` beside each view's PNG (default dir `tmp/parity/<time>`; it prints every path). It builds `out/` first, runs under the lock, and exits non-zero naming the surface and view when a view could not reach the surface, the part the surface names (`parity.inView`) is not mounted inside its transcript's visible box, or its transcript scrolls sideways (6 Nov 2026: a reply picture's chip spilled its path out of its figure cell); a report never carries a partial set as a pass.
  - The sideways check measures only rows the virtualized transcript mounted, so a surface whose point is one part of a reply names it: each `reply-*` showcase surface its heading, `reply-text` the row of pictures whose files are gone. `showInReply` scrolls with an upward wheel first, as a reader does: a programmatic scroll alone left the transcript following its end (ChatStream `pinned`), and a row measured after it pulled two views of `reply-text` back to the narrated run. Proven 6 Nov 2026 with `MarkdownImageFallback`'s `shrink-0` put back: `reply-text` failed sideways in all three views; restored, all pass.
- The surfaces are the responsive audit's (`e2e/responsive-surfaces.ts`); one with a `parity` session (`work`: a session with one answered turn, `bot`: the bot's with a duty and an open decision) can be captured: `sidebar`, `sidebar-family`, `session-idle`, `bot-bar`, `bot-call-preparing`, `bot-call-speaking`, `bot-call-hearing`, `bot-call-transcribing`, `composer-dictation-preparing`, `composer-dictation-downloaded` so far. `sidebar-family` (`remoteChild`, parity only: the audits skip it) first pairs B with A both ways, leaves a session B's bot opened on A running with only the bot's ended conversation naming its opener, and runs a session of A's own; it opens the bot's family and folds every other device section. The six voice surfaces (parity only) script the host's fake omp through `OMP_GUI_TEST_VOICE_FILE` (`voiceScript` in `e2e/fixture/rpc-ui.ts`: `prepare` holds a Korean model download at 42 % and leaves `transcribe_audio` unanswered; `holdTranscribe: true` leaves it unanswered with the model here; `transcript` is a Korean utterance and `replySeconds` a 60 s silent reply; `partials: false` is Whisper large-v3-turbo, offline, so `stt_stream_push` answers no running words; `holdStop: true` leaves `stt_stream_stop` unanswered, the call's "Transcribing…". A held reply is answered with no text once the script stops holding it, or at once by `stt_stream_cancel`) and give the page a tone for a microphone (`fakeMicrophone`: `bot-call-speaking` and `-transcribing` 1.2 s of floor then 1.5 s of sound, `-hearing` the sound for a minute, the dictation surfaces sound at once). `composer-dictation-preparing` (session `work`) turns omp's `stt.enabled` on with `set_setting`, starts a take with a click on the composer's mic (the toolbar's `Start dictation` button on this computer's window and on a paired computer's view of it, the empty Send slot's mic on the phone) and ends it with the toolbar's `Stop recording and insert text` button in all three views; `composer-dictation-downloaded` takes the same take past its download (`prepare` dropped, `holdTranscribe` set) and is ready once the bar says "Transcribing…" with no download left on it. A surface's `parity.after` puts back what its `open` changed on the host (here the script and `stt.enabled`) after each view's capture. A touched surface without one gets it in the same change, when its `open` works on a real window (a click on a real control); one driven through `window.__stores` (harness only) gets a real-window `open` first. Look at the three panels before reporting: a difference the change did not mean is a bug of the change.
- **Any set of parity surfaces passes in any order** (the bot's release gate runs the sheet before every release). The surfaces of one capture share its two apps, so each leaves the host as it found it or the harness reads the host afresh:
  - Every view reads its session from the host when it shows it (`hostSession` in `e2e/parity.e2e.ts`), never a path kept from the setup: `sidebar-family`'s setup renews the bot's conversation (`new_conversation`), and the bot surfaces after it opened the ended one (3 Nov 2026: `bot-call-transcribing` failed in all three views after `sidebar-family`, passed alone). The phone's tabs are kept per session file for the same reason.
  - The four call surfaces' `parity.after` (`endBotCall`) ends the call on its view and drops the voice script: a call stays on until its person ends it, and one left running drew its bar over `bot-bar` on this computer and the paired view, not on the reloaded phone.
  - What `sidebar-family`'s setup builds (B and A paired both ways, the bot's child running on A, A's own session running, the renewed conversation) and what the call surfaces said in the bot's conversation stay for the rest of the run: a capture after them shows them, by design.
  - The guard: `bun run parity -- --both-orders [--out <dir>]` captures every parity surface in one run and again in reverse order (`<dir>/forward`, `<dir>/reverse`) and fails when either fails: about 3 minutes with `out/` built (166 s on the 16 GB Mac mini, 3 Nov 2026). `bun run check:changed` runs it whenever the change picks `e2e/parity.e2e.ts` (a change to the sheet, its runner or `e2e/responsive-surfaces.ts`). A new parity surface passes it before it lands.

### agent-desktop in the transcript
- `mcp__agent_desktop_*` tools (the computer-use engine, `AGENT_DESKTOP_TOOL_PREFIX` in `resources/gui-bridge/modes/computer-use-contracts.ts`), direct or as `write xd://mcp__agent_desktop_*`, render through ONE renderer, `tools/renderers/AgentDesktopRenderer.tsx`, chosen in `getToolRenderer` by prefix before the generic `mcp__` fallback. `lib/agent-desktop.ts` models the call's JSON envelope once (`details.structuredContent`, else the first text block; never throws): a screenshot is the zoomable image, a snapshot, find or list is a `TreeView` of `role "name" @ref` rows (levels below the second folded, 400 rows at most), an action is one "Done · delivered…" line from `data.disposition`, a failure is code, message, suggestion and whether a retry is safe, `desktop_skills_get` a folded guide. A result that is no envelope falls back to `McpRenderer`. The row reads "Desktop · click @e5" with the `computer` tool's pointer icon (`getToolHeader`), and the live peek maps these tools to `data-surface="computer"` so the screenshot fills it as the built-in's does. Parity: transcripts reach a paired computer's window and the phone's `web.html` page through the same `MessageBubble`/`ToolCard` (`web/web-boot.ts` loads `main.tsx`), so none of this has a separate path. Pinned by `AgentDesktopRenderer.test.tsx` and the live-surface case of `ExecutionGroup.test.tsx`.
- A session that wanted agent-desktop and fell back to the built-in `computer` prelude carries one custom message `ompdesk-computer-use` (`parseComputerUseNotice`); `chat/ComputerUseNote.tsx` draws it as one muted line (reason: not installed, or the start error; plus "Settings › Agent behavior › Computer use"), dispatched in `MessageBubble` beside the memory and mail notes (`ComputerUseNote.test.tsx`, "computer use fallback line" in `MessageBubble.test.tsx`). Never a work-card note: only dedicated card types (`CARD_TYPES`) ride a work run.
