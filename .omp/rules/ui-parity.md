## UI parity

### Parity sheet: this computer, a paired computer, the phone
- **A UI change's final report attaches the parity sheet of every surface it touched** (the owner's rule): `bun run parity -- <surface…>` (`scripts/parity.ts`, `e2e/parity.e2e.ts`) captures the same session of one computer three ways (its own desktop window at 1280×860, another OMPdesk paired with it viewing that session from its section of the sidebar, and its phone page at 390×844 in Chromium), en light on the fake omp, and puts them side by side in `<dir>/<surface>/sheet.png` beside each view's PNG (default dir `tmp/parity/<time>`; it prints every path). It builds `out/` first, runs under the lock, and exits non-zero naming the surface and view when a view could not reach the surface, the part the surface names (`parity.inView`) is not mounted inside its transcript's visible box, or its transcript scrolls sideways; a report never carries a partial set as a pass.
  - The sideways check measures only rows the virtualized transcript mounted, so a surface whose point is one part of a reply names it: each `reply-*` showcase surface its heading, `reply-text` the row of pictures whose files are gone. `showInReply` scrolls with an upward wheel first, as a reader does, because a programmatic scroll alone leaves the transcript following its end (ChatStream `pinned`).
  - **`tool-agent-desktop`** (`reply` session) captures agent-desktop's transcript rendering: the fake omp's `fixture agent-desktop` turn (e2e/fixture/rpc-ui.ts) makes three stock-shaped `mcp__agent_desktop_desktop_*` calls (snapshot, type, screenshot) and answers "agent-desktop run finished."; `open` opens the turn's work card and all three step rows, then scrolls the snapshot tree to the top (three times, while the picture and the lazy renderer chunk settle row heights), `inView` is `[data-tool-agent-desktop="tree"]`. The audits' harness page has no such turn (`when: () => false`).
- The surfaces are the responsive audit's (`e2e/responsive-surfaces.ts`); one with a `parity` session (`work`: a session with one answered turn, `bot`: the bot's with a duty and an open decision, `bot-ended`: the newest conversation the bot ended, B renewing its bot first when it has none) can be captured: `sidebar`, `sidebar-family`, `sidebar-roster`, `session-idle`, `bot-bar`, `bot-ended`, `bot-call-preparing`, `bot-call-speaking`, `bot-call-hearing`, `bot-call-transcribing`, `composer-dictation-preparing`, `composer-dictation-downloaded` so far. `bot-ended` (parity only) opens the ended conversation as its owner does on this computer's window and a paired computer's view (the bot's row, then the conversation under the bot's earlier conversations, `[data-bot-past-open]`) and from the phone's tab list, and is ready at the note that stands where the composer was (`[data-bot-ended-note]`; no view waits for a composer there): its name reads "<name> · earlier conversation MM-DD". `sidebar-family` (`remoteChild`, parity only: the audits skip it) first pairs B with A both ways, leaves a session B's bot opened on A running with only the bot's ended conversation naming its opener, and runs a session of A's own; it opens the bot's family and folds every other device section. `sidebar-roster` (`hiddenRoster`, parity only) has B run a second session, "Parity roster", that each view opens once and leaves for the work session, so each holds a tab of it it does not show; a turn of it then starts two subagents, and the surface is ready only when that row's chip counts 2 with no click in all three views (the phone's capture loads its page afresh, so its tab list reads them). What it leaves running stays for the rest of the run. The six voice surfaces (parity only) script the host's fake omp through `OMP_GUI_TEST_VOICE_FILE` (`voiceScript` in `e2e/fixture/rpc-ui.ts`: `prepare` holds a Korean model download at 42 % and leaves `transcribe_audio` unanswered; `holdTranscribe: true` leaves it unanswered with the model here; `transcript` is a Korean utterance and `replySeconds` a 60 s silent reply; `partials: false` is Whisper large-v3-turbo, offline, so `stt_stream_push` answers no running words; `holdStop: true` leaves `stt_stream_stop` unanswered, the call's "Transcribing…". A held reply is answered with no text once the script stops holding it, or at once by `stt_stream_cancel`) and give the page a tone for a microphone (`fakeMicrophone`: `bot-call-speaking` and `-transcribing` 1.2 s of floor then 1.5 s of sound, `-hearing` the sound for a minute, the dictation surfaces sound at once). `composer-dictation-preparing` (session `work`) turns omp's `stt.enabled` on with `set_setting`, starts a take with a click on the composer's mic (the toolbar's `Start dictation` button on this computer's window and on a paired computer's view of it, the empty Send slot's mic on the phone) and ends it with the toolbar's `Stop recording and insert text` button in all three views; `composer-dictation-downloaded` takes the same take past its download (`prepare` dropped, `holdTranscribe` set) and is ready once the bar says "Transcribing…" with no download left on it. A surface's `parity.after` puts back what its `open` changed on the host (here the script and `stt.enabled`) after each view's capture. A touched surface without one gets it in the same change, when its `open` works on a real window (a click on a real control); one driven through `window.__stores` (harness only) gets a real-window `open` first. Look at the three panels before reporting: a difference the change did not mean is a bug of the change.
- **`sidebar-own-project`** (`crossProject`, parity only): B's work session opens "Parity cross-project" with `ompdesk_session_open {folder}` in another folder of B's (`seuoffice`) and leaves its turn running; `open` keeps only the section holding the work row, opens its family (waiting for the child's family row) and expands `seuoffice`; ready only when the child is a row of `seuoffice` too, in each view. **`sidebar-folder-missing`** and **`missing-folder-sheet`** (`folderMissing`, parity only): B's "Parity folder gone" answered one turn in `moda-office`, its tab closed and the folder was deleted (a turn of the work session afterwards makes main read the folders again); the first is ready when its row carries `data-folder-missing`, the second clicks the row, waits for the stored transcript in the sheet's body (`[data-missing-folder-sheet] .omp-transcript-row`) and is ready when its footer offers Archive (`[data-missing-folder-archive]`), closed by Escape after each view.
- **`model-picker`** and **`settings-model-role-picker`**: the composer's model picker opened by a click on the model chip (`[data-model-chip]`), and Settings › Models & accounts with the Default role's picker open (`#model-role-default`'s trigger; parity only). Both close with Escape after each view. They show every model as its provider's mark and compact name (composer.md › Toolbar, `compactModelLabels`), grouped by provider (`groupModelsByProvider`). Run with `OMP_GUI_TEST_LOGIN=1 OMP_GUI_TEST_MODELS=1 OMP_GUI_TEST_OPENROUTER=1` to capture them over Anthropic's and OpenRouter's look-alike models; without it the host lists the fixture model alone.
- **`card-ask-pictures`** (`work` session, parity only): each view sends the shown tab the prompt `fixture ask pictures` (`window.omp.rpc.commandForTab`), and the host's fake omp asks the card the bridge's ask form makes of a form of three drafts (composer.md › Ask forms): ready when the third picture of the figure row above the question is read (from the host in the paired and phone views); Escape answers it, which ends that turn.
- **`reply-usage`** (`reply` session, parity only): the reply session's agent-desktop run (`fixture agent-desktop`; no turn of its own, so every other reply surface sees the session it saw before) ends on a reply carrying every segment of the usage row (`chat/UsageRow.tsx`), and the sheet turns the row on for all three views (the host's and the paired computer's `displayPreferences.showTokenUsage` in their profiles; the phone's own device pref, set once after pairing: a phone's display switches are its own, never the host's). `open` brings the transcript back to its end with "Jump to latest" when a surface before scrolled it up (a scroll of its own left that button over the row), `inView` is `.omp-usage-row`. **The row is one line at every width** (the owner's rule): the row is its own size container, `DROP` hides one segment per width step, least useful first (first-token time, throughput, message time, cache, duration, input; model, output and cost always stay), the model's name gives way to an ellipsis, and the row's tooltip names every value. `e2e/usage-row.e2e.ts` pins it on the dev harness: one line and nothing past the row's edge from 320 to 768 px in English and Korean, every segment on a 1280 px window.
- **Any set of parity surfaces passes in any order** (the bot's release gate runs the sheet before every release). The surfaces of one capture share its two apps, so each leaves the host as it found it or the harness reads the host afresh:
  - Every view reads its session from the host when it shows it (`hostSession` in `e2e/parity.e2e.ts`), never a path kept from the setup: `sidebar-family`'s setup renews the bot's conversation (`new_conversation`), and the bot surfaces after it would otherwise open the ended one. The phone's tab on that file is read from the phone's own tab list each time, for the same reason.
  - The four call surfaces' `parity.after` (`endBotCall`) ends the call on its view and drops the voice script, because a call stays on until its person ends it and one left running draws its bar over later surfaces.
  - What `sidebar-family`'s setup builds (B and A paired both ways, the bot's child running on A, A's own session running, the renewed conversation) and what the call surfaces said in the bot's conversation stay for the rest of the run: a capture after them shows them, by design.
  - The guard: `bun run parity -- --both-orders [--out <dir>]` captures every parity surface and, as a second test of the same Playwright run, all of them again in reverse order (`<dir>/forward`, `<dir>/reverse`; side by side where the lock gives the run two workers) and fails when either fails. `bun run check:changed` runs it whenever the change picks `e2e/parity.e2e.ts`: a change to the sheet, to its runner `scripts/parity.ts` or to `e2e/responsive-surfaces.ts` (`PARITY_INPUTS` in `scripts/changed-specs.ts`). A new parity surface passes it before it lands.
  - **It stays green on main, so a red one is the change's.** A surface never relies on what an earlier one left:
    - The `reply` session's transcript is virtualized (rows around the view plus 8), so every `reply` surface names its turn first (`showTurn`: an upward wheel, then a view's height at a time until the turn's prompt is in the page), and a target is where a scroll put it only once it has stayed there for ten frames (`holdAtTop`), because rows above still measure.
    - A tab moves with its session (the bot's follows a renewed conversation), so the phone's tab on a file is read from the phone each time.
    - A surface that opens the side panel (e.g. `panel-diff`, `panel-files-viewer`) closes it in `parity.after` (`closePanelView`), because the work board docks there and folds the bot's board.
  - A view that fails saves what it showed as `<dir>/<surface>/<view>-failed.png` and names the file in its failure line: read the picture before running anything again.

### agent-desktop in the transcript
- `mcp__agent_desktop_*` tools (the computer-use engine, `AGENT_DESKTOP_TOOL_PREFIX` in `resources/gui-bridge/modes/computer-use-contracts.ts`), direct or as `write xd://mcp__agent_desktop_*`, render through ONE renderer, `tools/renderers/AgentDesktopRenderer.tsx`, chosen in `getToolRenderer` by prefix before the generic `mcp__` fallback. `lib/agent-desktop.ts` models the call's JSON envelope once (`details.structuredContent`, else the first text block; never throws): a screenshot is the zoomable image, a snapshot, find or list is a `TreeView` of `role "name" @ref` rows (levels below the second folded, 400 rows at most), an action is one "Done · delivered…" line from `data.disposition`, a failure is code, message, suggestion and whether a retry is safe, `desktop_skills_get` a folded guide. A result that is no envelope falls back to `McpRenderer`. The row reads "Desktop · click @e5" with the `computer` tool's pointer icon (`getToolHeader`), and the live peek maps these tools to `data-surface="computer"` so the screenshot fills it as the built-in's does. Parity: transcripts reach a paired computer's window and the phone's `web.html` page through the same `MessageBubble`/`ToolCard` (`web/web-boot.ts` loads `main.tsx`), so none of this has a separate path. Pinned by `AgentDesktopRenderer.test.tsx` and the live-surface case of `ExecutionGroup.test.tsx`.
- A session that wanted agent-desktop and fell back to the built-in `computer` prelude carries one custom message `ompdesk-computer-use` (`parseComputerUseNotice`); `chat/ComputerUseNote.tsx` draws it as one muted line (reason: not installed, or the start error; plus "Settings › Agent behavior › Computer use"), dispatched in `MessageBubble` beside the memory and mail notes (`ComputerUseNote.test.tsx`, "computer use fallback line" in `MessageBubble.test.tsx`). Never a work-card note: only dedicated card types (`CARD_TYPES`) ride a work run.
- A computer-use step that failed for a missing macOS permission (agent-desktop's `PERM_DENIED` in `FailedCall`, stock's "… permission is not granted" in `ComputerRenderer`/`EvalRenderer`) shows `chat/MacPermissionFix.tsx` under its error: each missing permission with **Allow…**, "Allowed. Ask the agent to try again." once the window regains focus with it granted, or a line saying to allow it on the host for a paired host's session. The same control is Settings › Agent behavior's two permission rows (control-tools.md › macOS Accessibility and Screen & System Audio Recording).
