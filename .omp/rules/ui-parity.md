## UI parity

### Parity sheet: this computer, a paired computer, the phone
- **A UI change's final report attaches the parity sheet of every surface it touched** (the owner's rule, 3 Nov 2026, after agents kept checking only their own window): `bun run parity -- <surface…>` (`scripts/parity.ts`, `e2e/parity.e2e.ts`) captures the same session of one computer three ways (its own desktop window at 1280×860, another OMPdesk paired with it viewing that session from its section of the sidebar, and its phone page at 390×844 in Chromium), en light on the fake omp, and puts them side by side in `<dir>/<surface>/sheet.png` beside each view's PNG (default dir `tmp/parity/<time>`; it prints every path). It builds `out/` first, runs under the lock, and exits non-zero naming the surface and view when a view could not reach the surface; a report never carries a partial set as a pass.
- The surfaces are the responsive audit's (`e2e/responsive-surfaces.ts`); one with a `parity` session (`work`: a session with one answered turn, `bot`: the bot's with a duty and an open decision) can be captured: `sidebar`, `sidebar-family`, `session-idle`, `bot-bar`, `bot-call-preparing`, `bot-call-speaking`, `bot-call-hearing`, `bot-call-transcribing`, `composer-dictation-preparing` so far. `sidebar-family` (`remoteChild`, parity only: the audits skip it) first pairs B with A both ways, leaves a session B's bot opened on A running with only the bot's ended conversation naming its opener, and runs a session of A's own; it opens the bot's family and folds every other device section. The five voice surfaces (parity only) script the host's fake omp through `OMP_GUI_TEST_VOICE_FILE` (`voiceScript` in `e2e/fixture/rpc-ui.ts`: `prepare` holds a Korean model download at 42 % and leaves `transcribe_audio` unanswered; `transcript` is a Korean utterance and `replySeconds` a 60 s silent reply; `partials: false` is Whisper large-v3-turbo, offline, so `stt_stream_push` answers no running words; `holdStop: true` leaves `stt_stream_stop` unanswered, the call's "Transcribing…". A held reply is answered with no text once the script stops holding it, or at once by `stt_stream_cancel`) and give the page a tone for a microphone (`fakeMicrophone`: `bot-call-speaking` and `-transcribing` 1.2 s of floor then 1.5 s of sound, `-hearing` the sound for a minute, the dictation surface sound at once). `composer-dictation-preparing` (session `work`) turns omp's `stt.enabled` on with `set_setting`, starts a take with a click on the composer's mic (the toolbar's `Start dictation` button on this computer's window and on a paired computer's view of it, the empty Send slot's mic on the phone) and ends it with the toolbar's `Stop recording and insert text` button in all three views; a surface's `parity.after` puts back what its `open` changed on the host (here the script and `stt.enabled`) after each view's capture. A touched surface without one gets it in the same change, when its `open` works on a real window (a click on a real control); one driven through `window.__stores` (harness only) gets a real-window `open` first. Look at the three panels before reporting: a difference the change did not mean is a bug of the change.
