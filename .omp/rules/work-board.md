- **Work card head, findings and steps** (`chat/ExecutionGroup.tsx`, pure rules in `chat/work/work-checks.ts`, `work-findings.ts`, `work-steps.ts`; design record `docs/work-card-design.md`):
  - A finished card's facts name what the run's shell commands checked instead of counting them. `checkKind` reads the command's own words (test runners, `tsc`/`check:types`, biome/eslint/ruff, `build`); the last run of each kind decides (`summarizeChecks`: "128 tests passed", "types OK", "build failed"); a non-zero `details.exitCode` fails even without stock's error flag; interrupted and unanswered runs are skipped and still count as commands. The test count is the runner's last printed summary (`testCounts`: vitest, jest, bun, pytest, cargo, go); other commands keep "N commands". Every fact stays in the head button's accessible name and in Copy summary; the head itself draws only its time, the step count and the token meter, and the verdict blocks, chips and agent lanes below carry every other fact.
  - **A backgrounded check has no verdict of its own** (stock bash `async: true` or auto-background: its result is only the "Backgrounded as job bg_N" notice, `details.async.state: "running"`). `workOutcomes` takes the job's settled outcome instead, from `backgroundJobVerdicts` (`chat/completion-events.ts`, memoized per committed message list): stock's `async-result` delivery (`details.jobs[].status`, the job's text split by `asyncJobResults`) or, when the agent waited, its `wait` result (`details.jobs[]` snapshot: `status`, `exitCode`, `resultText`/`errorText`; stock does not deliver a waited result again). Until one arrives the command is `unknown`, never a passed check (it once read "tests passed" and Tests ✓ while the job still ran or had failed). Pinned by the "backgrounded checks" cases of `work-checks.test.ts`.
  - **The card is one frame** (`.omp-execution-group`: border, radius, `overflow: clip`; no rule inside it, spacing separates the bands; opened, the steps sit inside on a `--sc-surface-1` well). A finished card reads, in `order`: the head (mark, headline, time, copy, **one step-count toggle**), the verdict blocks and chips, the agent lanes, the notes, the media (pictures, audio and video the run made or read, `WorkMedia`).
