## Panels, sidebar and work views

### Work board and work card

- **Work board** (`layout/work-board/WorkBoard.tsx`, rows `board-parts.tsx`, rules in `work-board.ts`, look in `styles/work-board.css`, design record `docs/work-board-design.md`; mounted per session pane by `SplitWorkspace`): a pane with at least 300 px (`WORK_BOARD_MIN_SPARE`) beside its transcript column (`workBoardFits`, measured by ResizeObserver; constants pinned to components.css by `work-board.test.ts`) docks this session's live state in that margin, 264 px wide, 320 px from 420 px of margin (`workBoardWidth`, set on the pane as `--omp-board-width`).
  - **A tablet held sideways docks it too** (the user's call; `workBoardDocks`, by the measured pane width, never a device check): the board docks at 264 px where the transcript column may narrow to keep at least 503 px (`WORK_BOARD_MIN_TRANSCRIPT`; `workBoardFitsNarrowed`: pane − board − gap − the column's paddings), in the web build's `tablet-landscape` shell and in a desktop window alike (a desktop pane docks from 830 px). 503 is the floor that still reads: at 500 px or less (`@container omp-toolbar (max-width: 500px)`; the toolbar is the box less its 2 px border) the model chip loses its name, so 503; at 504 the tables, diff paths, the work card's one-line head and the one-row toolbar fit (`work-board.test.ts` pins the floor to that rule). Every portrait tablet keeps the strip. Under touch the board's list declares `--hit-gap-y` (rows 1 px apart) so a docked row's reach never takes the next row's tap. Pinned by `work-board.test.ts`, `responsive-smoke.e2e.ts` (1366 and 1133 docked, 1024 strip), `mobile-shell.e2e.ts` (iPad Pro 11 sideways docked) and the responsive audit's 1133/1180/1366 landscape looks.
  - **One line per row, everywhere** (the user's rule): a lead mark, a label that truncates, one short figure; everything else (a subagent's step, tokens and model, a job's kind, an opened session's report, branch and cost in words, the goal's budget and time) is the row's tooltip. No row may widen the board: every row's text column clips (`min-w-0 overflow-hidden`), and the responsive audit's `overflow-sideways` (with `responsive-smoke`'s busy board, docked at 1600 and in the 390 strip) fails a board wider than its box. Sections cap their rows (`SECTION_CAP`: asks 3, running 4, children 3; the plan 5, `TODO_BOARD_ROWS`) and fold the rest into one "+N more" row that lists them on a tap (`CappedRows`).
  - Content, in order (four zones; `docs/work-board-design.md` "Round 2" says why): the head: the pulse and **one clause** that never wraps (it clips at 264 px): what needs the person ("3개 확인이 필요해요", warning), else what runs ("6개 실행 중"), else "Working…" (a child at work alone is working, never "Idle"), else what the last turn left ("작업을 마쳤어요 · 파일 7개 변경 +112 −37": the files, else the passing check, else what the branch has not landed, `quietOutcome`; "Idle" only when there is nothing to say), and, beside a needs-you clause, a "실행 6" figure.
    - **Every count comes from one `boardCounts` (`work-board.ts`)**: the head, Needs you's count and live region and the strip's collapsed label (`WorkBoardChip`) read it, so a figure is never stated twice with two values (needs = asks + waiting children; running = subagents and jobs, children at work apart). The pulse (`counts.pulse`, docked and in the strip, the strip's border too) is warning while a question, an approval or a waiting child is listed, else working while anything works (a parked or idle subagent is live but not at work: `workingSubagents`), else idle.
    - The board's name is only for screen readers; in the phone's strip the head draws no clause (the strip says it). Then a linked worktree's project; then one meters line where the subscription accounts' gauges (`AccountLimit.tsx` with `UsageGauges compact`, the same chip as the sidebar's usage lines: window name muted, a `--sc-space-1-5` gap that holds however narrow the bar, percent in tabular numerals; read as "5시간 한도 64%") and each metered provider's charge (`SessionSpendLine.tsx`) sit side by side and wrap only when they do not fit; addresses, resets and today's charge are tooltips. **The gauges always show** (the user's rule: the board is where the person looks while work runs; never hidden while the sidebar's usage block is expanded).
    - Section ids (store `stores/work-board.ts`): `needs`, `now`, `plan`, `current` (+ the Autopilot ones). Folds saved under earlier ids are dropped when read (`migrateCollapsed`), because mapping them would let one old fold fold a whole zone, and Needs you never starts folded (pinned by `stores/work-board.test.ts`). Section folds persist (pref `workBoardCollapsed`); rows leave through `usePresence`.
    - **Needs you** (`needs`; a warning-washed callout; the only live region; ⌥B `board.focus` lands on its first row, `lib/work-board-focus.ts`): a question or approval and a plan up for review (`data-board-waiting`; a tap focuses the docked card), and a session the agent opened that waits for an answer (`WaitingChildren`). Each row starts with a fixed-width word saying which (`RowTag`: Ask, Review, Child; "질문/검토/하위"), colour only as the state it names. In an Autopilot tab the run's own sections come next (its head and the `autopilot`, `cycles`, `followups`, `ship` sections, see Autopilot › Overview).
    - **Now** (`now`; titled "Child sessions" when nothing runs): the turn's live step (one row; finished calls are the work card's list; the turn meter's tok/s beside elapsed time), running subagents and background jobs, then the sessions the agent opened that are not waiting (`NowChildren`). A child row is the one row with a second line (the user's call): under the title, muted and truncated, what the child does (`childActivity` in `opened-children.ts`: a running one's step from main's live push, a waiting one's question, a finished one's report; no line when it said nothing). Finished children fold into one "n done" row.
    - **The Work tree is the `IconButton` (`WorkTreeButton`, `board-parts.tsx`; tooltip and aria-label "작업 트리 열기") in the head of the section that lists the children** (Now, or Needs you when every child waits), never a row; under touch it reaches 36 px without stealing a row's tap (`mobile.css`).
    - **Plan zone** (`plan`; titled "Goal & todos" / "Todos" / "Goal" by what it holds, never "Plan": that is the plan-mode card's word, and Needs you says "plan ready for review"). The header carries the one bar and "1/3", so the figure is stated once. The goal is the first line (the larger share of budget or time used, `goalMeta`, with its status) and is not repeated as its own section; then todos: every finished one folds into the one "n done" row wherever it sits (agents mark steps done out of order), a finished list's too; the unfinished ones listed in order (`todoBoard`, harness `?todos=mixed`).
    - Rows lead with a kind icon tinted by state (`KindMark`: subagent, job, child session, changed files, branch, preview, peers, memory, skills, check, goal); a dot only where state is all there is (todos, waiting). Running clocks are coarse (`elapsedLabel`: blank under 10 s, then 10 s steps, then minutes), so a busy board is not counters ticking.
    - **Current work** ("현재 작업", section `current`; `WorkBoardSession.tsx`, rules `session-board.ts`; the user's naming: rows read without a turn or session qualifier). Its rows:
      - What the turn's own edit tools changed (write, edit, ast_edit, apply_patch: the work card's `workOutcomes` file outcomes over the running turn's calls, the last finished turn's while idle, with its duration as the section's aside; no line when nothing changed), never git, so another session's work in a shared checkout never counts. The finished turn is kept as the tab's `useTodoStore.lastTurn`, so a board or strip mounted after it still counts it.
      - **plus the family's**: main's `agent_control_family_edits` (answered in `dispatchRpcCommand` beside `agent_control_review`, `AgentControl.familyEdits`; contract `src/shared/turn-edits.ts`) lists every session the board's session opened (an Autopilot run's cycle children included, in any window or on any paired device, so a phone viewing the opener counts them too), each with what its worktree branch has not landed (`get_git_unlanded`) and its newest turn's file-changing calls (`get_turn_edits`, local handler in `local/sessions.ts`: read back from its own transcript to the prompt that started the turn, a steer joining it, however long the turn, since a cut count would read as exact; nothing else of the turn leaves; a session not started, from its saved file). With the board's turn start as `since` it also lists each child main closed after its work landed since then (`agentClosedChildren`, which keeps the paired device a child ran on; its saved file read once, there). The renderer counts a member that runs or waits, or whose turn ended after the board's turn began: a worktree member's unlanded files, else its turn's edits through the same `workOutcomes`. A file counts once per computer and absolute path (`familyChanges`), its +/− lines summed over every session's edits of it; the tooltip names each session's share ("This session: …", "<child>: N files changed +a −r"). The family is read on mount and when its review rows (step, state) or the turn's start move, at most every 5 s. A session that only shares the checkout, or one hidden from agents, never counts; an older host that refuses `get_turn_edits` says nothing of its child.
      - On a session's own branch or worktree, what it has not landed yet (`get_git_unlanded`, `src/main/local/git/unlanded.ts`: the branch's commits and its uncommitted and untracked work against the merge-base with `origin/HEAD`, else `origin/main`/`origin/master`, else a local `main`/`master`; "main에 아직 없음 · 파일 N개 +a −r"; never on the default branch, where sessions share the working tree, nor on a detached HEAD), read on mount and once per settled turn. A cut list (`truncated`, 1000 files) reads "1000+" and leaves its lines out.
      - The newest test/build/lint/typecheck by the work card's own unresolved failures (`lastCheck` reads the standing failures of the turn's `workVerdict`: a failed check no later run of the same command fixed, else the newest). **a failed one is a plain row here, with the danger mark, never a need** (the owner's call: the agent fixes it and the work card's red verdict carries it); a tap reveals the call in the transcript; the quiet head never leads with it; `backgroundJobVerdicts`: a backgrounded one without its verdict is running, never passed.
      - Each running dev server by `devServerUrl` (a tap opens Preview; nothing opens by itself).
      - The sessions sharing the checkout (bridge frame `checkout_peers` + `get_checkout_peers`; never on a session alone): a row that expands in place into one line per peer, this worktree's and then the repository's other worktrees' (marked "other worktree"), each opening its session by `peerOpen`: a tab of this window is switched to, this computer's file goes to its owner (another window, a paired device's tab), a paired host's peer opens by the host's file; a session hidden from agents carries no file and does not open.
      - The memory the turn's recall attached, a row that expands in place (`data-board-expands`: the strip stays open for it).
      - The skills the newest turn read ("Skills · how · tdd", `skills`: the card's `WorkOutcomes.skills` over `useRunOutcome` of the newest turn's calls, subagents' included; a tap reveals the first read like a check row).
    - The dev harness's `?board=busy&family=1` (`familyEdits` in `dev-harness/fixture-board.ts`) is an orchestrator idle after its turn while its children work.
    - **The bot's board is its desk** (panels-sidebar.md › The bot's screen): in the bot's own pane (`BotTabContext`, components/bot) the board always shows, in this order: Needs you, Now, the bot's `tasks`, the plan, then its `routines`, `library`, `completed`, `activity`, `duties`, `channels` and `pastConversations` (its ended conversations, absent while none) sections (one line per row, a lazy chunk). It has no Current work section and no account gauges in its head (the sidebar shows the account; a metered charge of the bot's own session stays). Where the board cannot dock the strip stays on that pane with nothing running, labelled "Tasks N · Routines N". The bot's decisions are not Needs you rows: they dock above the composer.
    - **Each attention state is said once, in one word set, everywhere it belongs** (this bullet is the contract for `lib/attention.ts`, `hooks/use-session-attention.ts`, `src/main/tray-labels.ts` and `web/chrome.ts`). `lib/attention.ts` `sessionAttention({asks, childrenWaiting})` is the one source of the kind (`question`, `approval`, `childWaiting`; the session's first prompt first, then a waiting child) and of the count `boardCounts.needs` reads. **a failed check is none of them** (the owner's call: the person does not answer it, the work card's red verdict carries it); no surface says it as an attention (board, strip, title bar, sidebar row, tray, paired page).
      - Its words are `attention.*` ("Needs input", "Needs approval", "Child waiting"; "답변 필요", "승인 필요", "하위 세션 대기"), no wider than trunk's widest badge ("Needs approval"), so a sidebar row keeps its title. The tray (`tray-labels.ts`) and the judge's "Needs input · <tab>" banner (`notify.needsInput`) say the same, pinned by `native-labels.test.ts`. The board head and the strip say one thing by its word and several by their count ("3 need you", `attentionClause`); all in the warning tone. A phone's title bar hides the status word visually but keeps it for screen readers (clipped, not `display: none`), and the status carries it as its tooltip (`title`), with the warning icon.
      - **Priorities outside the board**: a question, an approval and a waiting child need the person and outrank work. **A session that is down** (connecting, restarting, failed, exited, held) says that on the title bar and its sidebar row over any attention kind (`isSessionDown`, `lib/tab-signal.ts`), as the tray's error does.
      - `hooks/use-session-attention.ts` `useSessionAttention` feeds the attached session's title bar badge, sidebar row (`data-signal` `waiting`), window title `!`, dock badge, tray and the paired page's badge (`web/chrome.ts`), so an idle session with a waiting child reads as needing the person, not "Ready". Every parent row in the sidebar says Child waiting when a session it opened waits, whichever session the window shows (`childrenWaitingOf` in `Sidebar.tsx`, the board's `openedChildRows`); that never makes it busy (delete stays allowed). A child's own family row waits only on its own question or approval: a finished child's error ending is the `failed` token, its unmerged work the `unmerged` one, neither an attention; a running child whose own child waits stays running. A paired device's hosted row only knows a prompt waits (`awaitingInput: true`) and reads as `question`.
      - The sidebar badge (`.omp-sidebar-approval`) is capped at 7.5rem as a guard: a longer translation ends in an ellipsis, the word staying its tooltip and the row's text. The goal bar has no "Needs your answer" chip and the dock island no "Needs you" count of the session's own asks (the prompt card says those); the island's sessions segment says "Child waiting N". A question is stated only where each place has its own job: the card, the composer placeholder pointing at it, the title bar and sidebar row (they reach other tabs and windows), the board head's clause, Needs you's count and its Ask row.
