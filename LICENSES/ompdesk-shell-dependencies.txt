OMPdesk's CEF shell: statically linked Rust crates

Cargo.lock SHA-256: 3c229bcf97b8e5e1bc3f9203a13dbcb5877120a7f01d66795687582654e3e9bf

The shell OMPdesk builds from its own Rust source in native/ompdesk/crates/shell (OMPdesk.exe / the app's
executable and its helper processes) links the crates below, among them the `cef` binding to the Chromium
Embedded Framework (libcef itself ships unmodified beside it; its notices are LICENSES.chromium.html and
LICENSE.cef.txt). Set: normal dependencies of every workspace crate for aarch64-apple-darwin and
x86_64-pc-windows-msvc, per native/ompdesk/crates/shell/Cargo.lock; build-only and proc-macro crates are not
linked. The workspace's own crates are OMPdesk's, under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE). Generated by
scripts/gen-crate-notices.ts; do not edit by hand.
